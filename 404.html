Common questions

How old do you have to be to start driver education in North Carolina?

+

Teens can start driver education at 14½ so they can apply for a limited learner permit at 15 after finishing the 30-hour classroom and 6-hour behind-the-wheel course.

What ages do you teach?

+

We teach students from 14 to 90 — teens, adults, new residents and seniors.

Where is Carolina Driving School located?

+

We are based in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, and serve Greensboro, High Point, Thomasville, Burlington, Graham and the wider Triad. We're licensed to teach anywhere in the state.

Do you pick students up from home?

+

Free home pick-up is for students who hold a learner's permit or a driver's license: adults, and teens taking remedial or additional driving lessons. Teens in the 30-hour classroom + 6-hour behind-the-wheel program come to our location; after they finish that program, we can pick them up.
